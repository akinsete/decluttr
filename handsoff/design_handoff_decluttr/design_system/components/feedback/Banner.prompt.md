Full-width nudge/notice row, optionally tappable and/or dismissable.

```jsx
<Banner tone="dark" icon={<PlayIcon />} title="Resume Photos" subtitle="12 of 20 left" onClick={resume} onDismiss={dismiss} />
<Banner tone="gradient" icon={<DupIcon />} title="3 duplicate photos found" subtitle="Tap to review now" onClick={reviewDuplicates} onDismiss={dismiss} />
<Banner tone="warning" title="Deleted contacts sit in Trash for 30 days." />
```
