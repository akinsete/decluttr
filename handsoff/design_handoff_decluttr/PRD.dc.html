Product Requirements Document

Decluttr

Clean up your contacts and photos, one swipe at a time — nothing leaves your phone.

Version

1.0

Platform

Flutter · iOS + Android

Status

Ready for build

1 · Summary

Decluttr is a mobile app for cleaning up a phone's Contacts and Photos libraries using a Tinder-style swipe gesture: swipe right to keep, swipe left to delete. The experience is fast, playful, and reassuring — nothing is ever deleted permanently on the first pass; everything routes through a 30-day Trash the user can restore from.

One product, one job: reduce the friction of tidying a phone from an hours-long chore to a series of 30-second sessions the user can do while waiting in line.

2 · Goals & non-goals

GOALS

  • Make decluttering fast and satisfying — swipe deck, streaks, progress, confetti payoff.
  • Make destructive actions safe & reversible — 30-day Trash, undo, "purges in N days".
  • Privacy-first framing: on-device, "nothing leaves your phone".
  • Handle duplicate contacts with a real merge flow.
  • Cover the full lifecycle: onboarding → core loop → trash → stats → settings → errors.

NON-GOALS (v1)

  • No cloud backup / cross-device sync.
  • No real account system beyond an optional sign-in stub (billing out of scope).
  • No desktop / web / tablet layouts — phone only (~402pt canvas).
  • No social sharing of activity.

3 · Design principles

  1. Reversible by default. Every delete goes to Trash first; every swipe can be undone.
  2. Numbers over adjectives. "11 waiting for you", not "some contacts need review".
  3. Calm, playful, pastel. Warm canvas, soft accents, rounded everything, spring motion.
  4. One primary action per screen. Generous whitespace; the next step is obvious.
  5. Touch-first. No hover; press = subtle scale(0.92). Hit targets ≥ 44pt.
  6. Sentence case everywhere except tracked uppercase eyebrows and KEEP/DELETE stamps.

4 · The core loop

STEP 1

Pick a batch

a photo month or contact group

STEP 2

Swipe

right = keep · left = delete · tap = detail

STEP 3

Summary

confetti + kept/deleted counts

Deleted items land in Trash (restore or purge). Completed sessions build a streak and feed the progress stats on Home.

5 · Feature requirements

Onboarding

Splash → Welcome → Get started → single "How it works" walkthrough (animated demo card + keep/delete/tap hints) → Home. Permissions are lazy — requested on first use of a Home card, not up front.

Home

Greeting (first-time vs returning), hero heading, two module cards (Contacts blue, Photos pink) with count subtitles, plus streak + progress cards for returning users. Frosted bottom dock: Home · Trash · Settings.

Permissions

Dedicated Contacts/Photos screens: floating illustration, bulleted rationale, "Allow access" → batch picker, "Not now" → Home. Denials route to the matching error state.

Batch picker

Photos grouped by month; contacts by smart group (Recently Added, No Phone, No Email, Duplicates, Old, Unknown Names, Work, Family & Friends). Tap starts a session; Duplicates opens the merge flow. Cleared tabs show an empty state.

Swipe session

Draggable card deck with live KEEP/DELETE stamps (threshold ±110px), tap-for-detail sheet, circular Keep/Delete buttons + Undo, progress bar and N/total counter. Empty deck → summary.

Duplicate contacts — merge review (key feature)

Distinct from keep/delete. Shows the two source records side by side (iPhone / iCloud / Google / Work) with a reason chip and an "After merge" preview (union of phone + emails). Three actions: Merge, Keep both, Delete one. Progress "N of M" → summary "X merged · Y kept · Z removed".

Trash

Photos + Contacts tabs, count & reclaimable storage, multi-select → Restore (with confirm) or Delete forever. Every item "purges in N days" (30-day window). Empty states per tab.

Error states (key feature)

One screen, four variants: unable to load photos, unable to access contacts, something went wrong, permission changed — each with an icon, title, message, and primary/secondary actions.

Streak & settings

Streak screen with week strip + "Last 5 weeks" heatmap. Settings: Go Premium, Preferences (Appearance, Haptics, Notifications), Account (sign in, delete), Rate / Share.

6 · State model

The app is a single state machine keyed on screen. Model these as repositories/providers backed by the platform Contacts/Photos APIs:

  • Navigation: splash, welcome, walkthrough, permissions, batch pickers, swipe, duplicates, trash, streak, settings, sign-in, error.
  • Permissions: contactsGranted, photosGranted (lazy).
  • Swipe: deck, total, kept, deleted, drag offset, tutorialSeen, undo history.
  • Batches: clearedPhotos / clearedContacts maps.
  • Duplicates: index, merged, kept, deleted counters.
  • Trash: items, counts, active tab, select mode, selection, restore confirm.
  • Prefs: hapticOn, notifOn, signedIn · Activity: hasActivity drives first-time vs returning.

7 · Success metrics

  • Activation: % new users completing ≥1 session first sitting.
  • Depth: items reviewed / session; batches cleared / week.
  • Retention: streak length; D1/D7 return.
  • Safety: restore rate from Trash.

8 · Open questions
